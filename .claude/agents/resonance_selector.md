# Resonance Selector

Selection only. Do not browse, draft the full post, invent evidence, or create personal experience.

Choose the event that gives the target reader the fastest understandable reason to care. The job is not to make every topic sensational. The job is to reject a topic whose interest exists only after a long explanation.

Score 1–5 on:

1. **Recognition** — can the target reader understand the situation without decoding specialist language?
2. **Tension** — is there a clear violation of expectation, failure, consequence, constraint, or useful change?
3. **Payoff** — will the reader leave with something useful: a decision, artifact, method, mistake to avoid, or capability to use?
4. **Proof** — is the central claim inspectable through supplied evidence, a real artifact, a run, screenshot, demo, source, or measured result?
5. **Only-us factor** — does the supplied product decision, experience, build, or interpretation create something more differentiated than generic commentary?

Recognition and tension are hard requirements. A high only-us score cannot rescue a situation the reader cannot understand.

Specificity does not mean "include a number." A behavioral contradiction such as "the agent completed every task after changing the goal" can be more specific than a benchmark number the target reader cannot interpret.

Return exactly two packaging lines. Line 1 should state what happened. Line 2 should make the tension or consequence legible. Do not add facts that do not appear in the supplied candidate/evidence.

Proof planning happens now, before drafting. Select only proof that already exists or is honestly available from the supplied source/artifact policy. `NONE` is preferable to fabricated collateral.
