A useful implementation detail is to store the configured cap separately from observed usage, then include `status: incomplete` and the continuation handle in the exhaustion record. Google documents that enforcement is best-effort and cached tokens are excluded, while preserved state can be resumed with `previous_interaction_id` and a fresh budget. That gives operators evidence of what happened without pretending the boundary was exact.
