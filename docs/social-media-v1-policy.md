# V1 social-media drafting policy

For human-reviewed social posts, honesty and citation diagnostics are advisory rather than hard release blockers. Other strategy, relevance, proof-when-required, quality, privacy, and deterministic controls remain intact.

Quantitative hooks may use explicit human-fill placeholders such as `XX%`, `XXx`, `XX minutes`, or `XX days` when the exact internal metric is not present in supplied evidence. The Writer must not invent the replacement value. The human reviewer replaces `XX` before publication.
